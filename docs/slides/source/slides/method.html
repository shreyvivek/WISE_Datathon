How it works. A matching engine finds comparable applications. That becomes structured case evidence: financial differences, stated denial reasons and missing information. IBM Granite turns it into an explanation, and every sentence is checked against the facts. A human reviewer verifies and decides. Matching is two-stage: hard filters first, then feature-specific tolerances. And it is blind by construction: demographics live in a separate table and are only joined after matching.
