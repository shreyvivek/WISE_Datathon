An exploratory model sensitivity analysis, trained with XGBoost inside the LinuxONE container. We predict the historical decision from finances, lender and county, with and without demographics. Without them, the model under-predicts denials for Black applicants, 11.5 percent against 13.1 actual; adding demographics closes the gap, while overall accuracy barely changes. So the public fields do not fully explain the differences. But this does not show that a lender used race: credit scores and other inputs are missing, and omitting race does not remove proxies.
