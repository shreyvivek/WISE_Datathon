Before any matching, denial rates differ a lot: 13.1 percent for Black applicants, 8.6 for Asian, 4.7 for White applicants. But raw rates mix finances, lenders and places, so they are not an adjusted comparison. That is exactly why we match on finances first. Very small groups are suppressed.
