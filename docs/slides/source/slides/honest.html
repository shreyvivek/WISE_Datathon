SYNTHETIC: 200 INJECTED NEAR-COPIES AMONG 2,600 REAL DENIALS · MEAN OF 5 SEEDS

06 / 16

We tested honestly. We injected 200 near-copies of approved applications as fake denials among the 2,600 real ones, over five random seeds, and asked which method ranks them first. A global nearest-neighbour search finds none, so the lender constraint is essential. But a plain same-lender similarity ranking does as well or better than our priority score, so we do not claim an accuracy win. Our value is evidence quality and workflow: tolerances, missing-data handling, the reason check and the audit trail. We did not measure review time, and real wrongful denials have no ground truth.
