Exploring the data: for most debt-to-income and loan-to-value bands, denied and approved applications overlap, which is why comparisons are meaningful. But 35.6 percent of denials have a debt-to-income ratio of 50 or more, against 0.7 percent of approvals. Where no approved application looks like the denied one, SecondLook says insufficient comparable evidence instead of forcing a comparison: only 6.5 percent of denials with DTI above 60 have a comparable approval.
